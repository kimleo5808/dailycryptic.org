# About dailycryptic

`dailycryptic` began as a **daily cryptic** and **minute cryptic** clue site, and that is still its core: one short cryptic clue a day, with progressive hints, an answer check and an explanation you can learn from. Around that core we have added guides, solving tools and daily help for the other word and logic puzzles our readers play.

## What You Will Find Here

- **Daily cryptic clues** — one original **minute cryptic** clue every day, written for this site, with a date-based archive and practice sets by difficulty and clue type
- **Cryptic learning guides** — a beginner guide, clue types, indicators, abbreviations, misleading words and a glossary
- **Solving tools** — a cryptic clue solver, crossword word finder, anagram solver, Wordle solver and 5-letter word lists
- **Daily puzzle hints and answers** — Wordle, Connections, Strands, Spelling Bee, Pips and Letter Boxed, plus the LinkedIn games (Queens, Zip, Tango, Pinpoint and Crossclimb)
- **Practice games** — Wordle Unlimited, Quordle, Connections, Strands and unlimited cryptic clues

## What We Publish for the Daily Cryptic

- Publish one **minute cryptic** clue every day as our **daily cryptic**
- Offer **progressive hints** for guided solving
- Provide **answer checking** and clear explanation for each clue
- Maintain a **date-based archive** so players can practice older clues

## How Hints Work

Each clue includes 4 hint levels, revealed one at a time:

1. **Level 1** - broad solving direction
2. **Level 2** - mechanism hint (for example anagram or container)
3. **Level 3** - structure guidance
4. **Level 4** - targeted help

The daily hint pages for other puzzles follow the same idea: the hints are listed before the full answer, so you can stop as soon as you have the nudge you need.

## Where Our Answers Come From

- **Cryptic clues** are written for dailycryptic. Every clue ships with its answer, clue type, difficulty and an explanation of how the wordplay works.
- **New York Times puzzles** (Wordle, Connections, Strands, Spelling Bee, Pips and Letter Boxed) are covered after each day's official puzzle is released. The answers are the official ones; the hints, breakdowns and archive pages are ours.
- **LinkedIn games** answers are compiled each morning and cross-checked wherever more than one source is available. If sources disagree, or an answer cannot be read with confidence, the page shows a "being verified" notice instead of a guess.

## Editorial Standards

### Clue Quality

- Natural surface reading
- Valid cryptic construction
- Clear split between definition and wordplay

### Difficulty Balance

- Balanced difficulty across the week
- Short sessions suitable for daily practice
- Explanations designed for learning, not only checking

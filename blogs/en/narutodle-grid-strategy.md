You guess Baki. His village lights up green, and his nature types turn yellow. Baki has Wind and Lightning. Which one earned you that yellow tile? If you play Wordle, you might instinctively pencil in Wind the way you'd pencil in a yellow letter. Here, either nature could explain the result.

In Classic mode, each name you enter gets compared with the mystery Naruto character across nine traits. The daily board and its rules are on [Narutodle](https://narutodle.app/). The short practice round below uses a hand-picked set of candidates, so you can try the deduction before opening today's game.

## Read each tile as a clue

A green **Village** tile on Baki's row gives you a place to start. The game lists Baki under Sunagakure, the Hidden Sand, so write “Sand” beside the empty answer. A character from another village might wear a familiar uniform or turn up in the same story arc. They still have to pass that village clue.

Now look at **Nature Types**. A green tile means the whole set matches. Yellow means the two sets have something in common and differ somewhere. Red means they share nothing. With Baki's yellow, a Wind-only character fits. So does a character with Lightning and Water. You need another piece of evidence to tell those possibilities apart.

![Two illustrated tiles show an exact village match and a partial match for Baki's Wind and Lightning natures.](/images/blog/narutodle-grid-strategy/village-and-natures.webp)

## Try a three-name shortlist

Suppose these are the only three names left on your scratchpad. Baki was your guess, and you have just those two clues from his row. Who can still be the answer?

| Candidate | Village | Nature types in the game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Darui | Hidden Cloud | Lightning, Water |
| Kankurō | Hidden Sand | Earth, Fire |
| Temari | Hidden Sand | Wind |

<summary>Reveal the deduction</summary>

**Temari** fits both tiles. Darui shares Baki's Lightning, but his village would have turned red. Kankurō gives you the right village, but neither Earth nor Fire overlaps with Baki's pair. Temari gives you green for the Sand and yellow for the Wind she shares with Baki. Cover either column in the table and two names look plausible again.

## Use a guess to test one nature

On a full board, plenty of names may still fit. A character with a single nature makes a handy next guess because you know exactly what you're testing. Try Temari to ask one question about Baki's yellow tile.

- **If her nature tile is red**, cross out Wind. Lightning must account for the overlap on Baki's row.
- **If it's yellow**, write down Wind. The answer has at least one more nature, and that extra nature is still a mystery.
- **If it's green**, the mystery character's full nature set is Wind alone. Use the other columns to separate the Wind users.

## Break a tie with the debut arc

If two names still fit your notes, look back along the Debut Arc column. An up arrow places the answer's first appearance later than that guess. A down arrow places it earlier. An earlier guess with an up arrow and a later guess with a down arrow give you a window. A character who *returns* during that window can still be ruled out if they first appeared before it.

You can practise the same decision in [Wordle Unlimited](/wordle-unlimited). Suppose your last two possibilities are **SHORE** and **SCORE**. They share four letters, so the useful question is what goes in the second square. In Narutodle that question might be “Does the character have Wind?” If you know what you're testing before you submit, even a miss tells you which name to cross out.

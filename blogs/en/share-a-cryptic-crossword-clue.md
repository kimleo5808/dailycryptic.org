You're stuck on 12 Across. Your friend would enjoy the clue, but they're still doing the Down clues and your grid would give half of them away. Send the clue on its own. With the wording, the answer length and one well-chosen question, the two of you have enough to solve it together.

## What to include in the message

Here's an original clue you can try on a friend. Imagine one of your Down answers has already given you an R in the second square. You could send this message exactly as it stands.

> 12 Across. Bird spotted in new rental (4)
>
> I've got `?R??` from a crossing. Could you point me towards the wordplay? I'd still like to find the bird myself.

“Bird spotted in new rental” is worth copying word for word. If you retell it as “bird seen in a rented house”, it still describes much the same scene, but the letters your friend needs have disappeared.

Keep the numbers in brackets, too. Solvers call this the *enumeration*. A six-letter entry marked `(3,3)` asks for two words, while `(6)` asks for one. For crossing letters, type a question mark for each empty square. If that R comes from a guess, say so. Your friend can then decide how much weight to give it.

## How to photograph just one clue

![Two index cards cover the neighbouring clues, leaving “12 Across  Bird spotted in new rental (4)” visible.](/images/blog/share-a-cryptic-crossword-clue/one-clue-crop.webp)

On a printed puzzle, lay one card above the clue and one below it. Move the lower card down if the clue wraps onto a second line. Hold the phone parallel to the paper and tap the print to focus. Crop away the grid, then open the saved image at the size your friend will see. Can you read the last bracket without zooming?

If your friend can't open an iPhone photo, check the filename. For a file ending in `.heic`, select the cropped photo in [HEICHub’s HEIC to PNG converter](https://heichub.com/), convert it in your browser and send the downloaded PNG. Paste the clue underneath so your friend can copy the letters into a reply.

## If you're asking in a group chat

Put the puzzle name, date or number, and clue number in the first message. “Anyone up for a hint on today's 12 Across?” lets people who are still solving decide whether to join in. Put the clue and your crossings in the thread, or send them directly to whoever offers to help. That keeps a well-meant reply from putting the answer in everyone's notification preview.

Tell the group where you're stuck. “Which end is the definition?” invites a one-word pointer. “I've filled the squares, but where does the R come from?” invites an explanation. For another clue to trade with a friend, pick one together from the [Minute Cryptic archive](/minute-cryptic).
